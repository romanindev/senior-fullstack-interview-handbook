# Interview Strategy

Senior-level interviews usually evaluate more than technical knowledge.

They evaluate how you think, communicate, prioritize, and make engineering decisions.

## What interviewers usually look for

Interviewers often look for:

- Strong fundamentals
- Practical experience
- Clear communication
- Ability to explain trade-offs
- Ownership mindset
- Debugging ability
- Architecture thinking
- Collaboration skills
- Risk awareness
- Product awareness

## Strong answer structure

A strong answer usually follows this pattern:

1. Direct short answer
2. Practical explanation
3. Trade-offs
4. Example from real work
5. Edge cases or risks
6. Summary

## Example structure

```text
The short answer is...
In practice, I would...
The trade-off is...
A common mistake is...
In production, I would also consider...
```

## Avoid

Avoid answers that are:

- Too theoretical
- Too long
- Tool-driven without reasoning
- Overconfident
- Missing trade-offs
- Missing production context
- Based only on memorized definitions

## Senior-level mindset

A senior engineer should not only answer "how".

A senior engineer should also answer:

- Why this approach?
- What are the trade-offs?
- What can go wrong?
- How would we monitor it?
- How would we roll it back?
- How would other teams use it?
- How would we maintain it over time?

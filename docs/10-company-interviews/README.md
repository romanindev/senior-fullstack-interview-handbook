# Company Interview Notes

This section contains public, ethical, vendor-neutral notes about interview preparation for different types of companies and interview styles.

## Purpose

The goal of this section is to help readers understand how interview focus may differ across company types, such as:

- Product companies
- SaaS companies
- Developer tools companies
- Infrastructure companies
- Cloud platforms
- Fintech companies
- E-commerce platforms
- Enterprise software companies

## Important rules

Do not add:

- Confidential interview questions
- Private company information
- Leaked interview materials
- NDA-protected content
- Personal candidate-specific notes
- Unsupported claims about a company

All content must be based on:

- Publicly available information
- General interview preparation patterns
- Ethical and reusable guidance
- Clearly stated assumptions

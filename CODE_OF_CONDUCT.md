# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want this project to be a respectful, inclusive, and constructive space for contributors.

## Expected behavior

Examples of positive behavior include:

- Being respectful and professional
- Giving constructive feedback
- Welcoming new contributors
- Focusing on ideas, not people
- Assuming good intent

## Unacceptable behavior

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- Harassment or discrimination
- Personal attacks
- Trolling or insulting comments
- Publishing private information
- Disruptive behavior

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove comments, close issues, reject pull requests, or block contributors who violate this code of conduct.
